# The card pilot, 2026-11-06

**What it is for:** to size `tarkir-dragonstorm-decks`' five-decks row and to
check the census against cards written, not read. Twenty cards were drawn and
each was written against the tree at #313's merge as far as the surface allowed,
in a throwaway crate outside the repo: 17 in whole or in part, 10 of those played
on a board, and 3 with no clause that could be written. None is registered, and
the crate was not kept, since no card lands in the tree. A dated record: the gap
census at step 13 replaces it.

**How they were drawn:** `pilot_pick.py`, seed 20261106, four from each list,
skipping registered cards, spread across verdicts. The census keeps counts, not
each card's verdict, and re-running it needs a Forge clone, so a card's verdict
here is a read of its Oracle text (`pilot_fetch.py`) for the rows the census
names, and `plain` for none. Over the 385 cards: 233 plain, 38
`information-model`, 34 `casting-from-other-zones`, 21 `remaining-costs`, 17
any-color, 16 modal, 15 copies and 11 combat. The draw favors the rare verdicts
on purpose, so **no share below describes the decks**.

**Read together:** 3 writable, 15 blocked by a row the route names, and 2 with a
gap no row owned, filed now as items 389 and 390 and slotted to the decks row.

**By verdict.** The three `plain` cards, the nearest to the census's "authorable",
came to one writable, one blocked and one gap, against the seven of twenty
`README.md`'s authoring check passed. Three cards bound nothing. Of the other 17,
two are writable, 14 blocked and one a gap.

**Reading missed two things the boards caught.** Noxious Gearhulk looked blocked
by menace alone, and Bone Devourer looked writable, and each compiled and was
refused at resolution.

**Loops:** none of the twenty can close a mandatory loop (CR 104.4b). Twenty cards
cannot rule that out, so `loop-detection`'s question stays the gap census's.

**A row to come reshapes the text of 9 of the 20**, most of them mechanically. That
is the case for the route's order: designs before cards.

**Where the cards unlock**, counting the last row each needs: 3 today, 4 more
through step 9, 1 by step 11, 6 by step 14, 4 by step 15, and the 2 gaps with the
decks. Cards could arrive steadily, not all at step 17.

**The time:** 28 minutes by the shell's clock for all twenty, after the first
build, with no review. Six whole cards took about a minute each, so what costs is
reading the surface and what the review asks.

**Lines:** registered cards run 18 lines of code each and 47 with comments, carry a
median of two rulings, and a test in the phase files runs about 35 lines. A bare
card and one test is about 60 lines; a commented card with two or three tests is
about 140. That is the five-decks row's range in `roadmap-v2.md`'s Sizing section.

**What the census did not see:**

- Its 373 is `e:tdc`. The lists hold 385, and the 12 others, printed in `tdm`,
  include two Omen cards (Stormshriek Feral, Whirlwing Stormbrood) the route's
  step 16 did not name.
- 21 deck cards search a library, and `information-model` is where they sit: a row
  that reads as hiding information holds the search instruction itself.
- Three of the twenty need a read or a word no row held, and the census named 21
  mechanics of that kind. Whether that is one card in ten is the gap census's to
  say: this draw cannot.
