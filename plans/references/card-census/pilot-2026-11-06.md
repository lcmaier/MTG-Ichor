# The card pilot, 2026-11-06

**What it is for:** to size `tarkir-dragonstorm-decks`' five-decks row and to
check the census against cards written, not read. Twenty cards were written
against the tree at #313's merge in a throwaway crate outside the repo. None
is registered and none landed. A dated record: the gap census at step 13
replaces it.

**How they were drawn:** `pilot_pick.py`, seed 20261106, four from each list,
skipping registered cards, spread across verdicts. The census keeps counts, not
each card's verdict, and re-running it needs a Forge clone, so a card's verdict
here is a read of its Oracle text (`pilot_fetch.py`) for the rows the census
names, and `plain` for none. Over the 385 cards: 233 plain, 38
`information-model`, 34 `casting-from-other-zones`, 21 `remaining-costs`, 17
any-color, 16 modal, 15 copies and 11 combat. The draw favors the rare verdicts
on purpose, so **the shares below describe this draw, not the decks**.

**What counts as writable:** it compiled and a board played it and asserted
what its text says. Seven cards reached a board. Blockers on the other thirteen
were read off the type surface, each a named missing arm or instruction, and
the census's own warning (`README.md#what-it-measures-and-how-far-to-trust-it`)
held: Bone Devourer compiles and is refused only at resolution.

**Read together:** 3 writable, 15 blocked by a row the route names, and 2 with a
gap no row owns. Three in twenty sits inside the census's corrected 9–27%.

**Loops:** none of the twenty can close a mandatory loop (CR 104.4b). Zero in
twenty still allows about one card in seven, so `loop-detection`'s question stays
the gap census's.

**A row to come reshapes the text of 9 of the 20**, most of them mechanically. That
is the case for the route's order: designs before cards.

**Where the rows' cards unlock**, counting the last row each needs: 3 today, 4
more through step 9, 2 by step 11, 5 by step 14, 4 by step 15, and the 2 gaps
when something owns them. Cards could arrive steadily, not all at step 17.

**The time:** six cards written with eight boards in six minutes by the shell's
clock, after the first build and a read of the authoring surface, about a minute
a card, with no review. Registered cards run 18 lines of code each and 47 with
their comments, and carry a median of two rulings. Those make the row's 60–140
lines a card with tests, in `roadmap-v2.md`'s Sizing section.

**What the census did not see:**

- Its 373 is `e:tdc`. The lists hold 385, and the 12 others, printed in `tdm`,
  include two Omen cards (Stormshriek Feral, Whirlwing Stormbrood) the route's
  step 16 did not name.
- 21 deck cards search a library, and the census's `information-model` count is
  where they sit: a row that reads as hiding information holds the search
  instruction itself.
- Two gaps in twenty suggests some 35 of the 378 cards need a facility no row
  owns. The census found 21 mechanics of that kind.
