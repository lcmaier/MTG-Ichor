What an Entry Writes Changes What Applies Next

RG made how a permanent enters one shape. CR 614.1c's "enters with" is state and "enters as" is characteristics, and one constructor builds the entering permanent for the performer and for the CR 614.12 look-ahead. It also gave the CR 616.1 loop a table of what each write can change. The review lost the thread between the design and the new code in pipeline.rs. So here are three boards, walked read by read from the records the engine's trace sink wrote, and the table of what each read consults.

Tests: mtgsim/tests/phase_rg_integration_test.rs, and RE-5's a_multiplier_beside_two_counter_writers_makes_their_order_observable. Every step below is a record in a traced run of that board at this commit.

The map

An entry is decided in a loop, CR 616.1's, and each pass through it can write something on the entering permanent: counters, a status, a copy, an edit. The whole phase is about what those writes do to the next pass, because the next pass asks again which effects apply (CR 616.1f), against the permanent as it would exist after the writes so far (CR 614.12). Trace A shows where the writes live. Trace B shows a write making a new effect applicable. Trace C shows a write switching an effect off.

Trace A — a Bears enters under Master Biomancer, then Humility arrives

Master Biomancer: each other creature you control enters with additional +1/+1 counters equal to Biomancer's power, and as a Mutant in addition to its other types. The counters are "enters with": state, which changes later like any state. The Mutant is "enters as": a characteristic the entry fixes. Before RG the engine had counters and no Mutant.

  1. change_zone(Bears, Battlefield, Returned) → entry_proposal builds EnterBattlefield { mods: EnterMods::NONE }, one batch of one member state. The proposal starts empty. Before RG, default_enter_mods seeded it with CR 110.5b's status and CR 306.5b's counters. Now the only seed is an instruction's own "tapped", which is not an effect (Archelos's second ruling).
  2. apply_replacements, iteration 1: EntryFrame::new, computed on first use. gather's source 1a asks frame.frame_of(Bears) → compute_as_entering → Lookahead::new → PermanentState::entering(game, Bears, 0, &NONE, next, next + 1): the would-be permanent, timestamped after everything on the board frame. The pass has it as a member: a 2/2 creature with no ability of its own (trace record 4, "membership": "entering").
  3. The battlefield sweep: Biomancer is in replacement_ability_sources → compute_characteristics(Biomancer) board → its static replacement, over "other creatures you control". set_affects reads the Bears' frame frame: a creature, player 0's, not Biomancer. One candidate, so decided: "single". The engine never prompts with fewer than two.
  4. Apply. evaluate_enter_template: the counters' amount is Biomancer's power, read off Biomancer's frame board (2), and the edit is Types(add Mutant). strip_prohibited_counters finds no "can't have counters". EnterMods::merge: counters: [+1/+1 × 2], edits: [Mutant] state. Biomancer's instance joins CR 614.5's applied set.
  5. Iteration 2: a new EntryFrame, because clause (1) of CR 614.12 counts the replacements already applied. PermanentState::entering now puts the two counters on the would-be entity and the edit in its entered_as. The pass's Board::seed calls EnteredAsNote::new, which lowers the edit once, to (Layer 4, AddSubtype(Mutant)). Layer 4 applies it at the Bears' timestamp, and layer 7c the counters: a 4/4 Mutant frame (record 7). gather: Biomancer's instance is applied, so there are no candidates and the loop ends.
  6. Perform: place_on_battlefield → PermanentState::entering(self, Bears, 0, mods, object_timestamp, first_counter). It is the same function as step 5, with the timestamps the move and the placement allocated. The entity carries entered_as.edits = [Mutant]. One epoch bump, then the announcements (records 9–10).
  7. has_subtype(Bears, Mutant) → compute_characteristics → pass_into_memo → run_pass. Board::seed notes the Bears' entered_as state. At layer 4, applications_in_layer pushes Kind::Own { AddSubtype(Mutant) } with Tiebreak::EntryEdit(0) at the Bears' timestamp, after the Bears' own static rows there. It is a Mutant, and no registry row exists for it.
  8. Humility arrives, player 1's: layer 6 "creatures lose all abilities", layer 7b "base 1/1". Biomancer's frame board has no abilities (record 14, a 1/1), so its replacement ability is gone and a creature entering now would get neither counters nor a Mutant. The Bears are still a Mutant. The edit comes from the Bears' own entered_as, and it is no ability's effect, so nothing that removes abilities can reach it.
Why state, and not a registry row. A row has a source ability and a duration, and it exists only while that source still has the ability (CLAUDE.md, "registry membership is not effect existence"). Here the source would be Biomancer's ability, so the Mutant would end when Biomancer left or, as in step 8, lost its abilities. CR 614.1c makes the Mutant how the creature entered. It lives on the permanent and leaves with it (CR 400.7), and a copy of the permanent doesn't get it (CR 707.2's last sentence). Applied at the permanent's timestamp at layer 4, it is ordered like any layer 4 effect: a later "set subtypes" applies over it.
Before RG, the look-ahead and the performer each built the entering permanent their own way, and they had drifted: the performer wrote a spell's X after the fact, so the frame could predict a permanent the performer would not place. Step 2's and step 6's single constructor is what makes the frame a prediction of the performer and not a copy of it.

Trace B — Adaptive Shimmerer under Master Biomancer, beside Doubling Season

Shimmerer enters with three +1/+1 counters, which is its own ability. Biomancer adds two more. Doubling Season doubles counters an effect would put on a permanent you control, and it can apply to an entry only once the entry carries counters (CR 616.2: it becomes applicable after a member applies). Scripted to reach 8, the outcome main could not.

  1. Iteration 1: EntryFrame → the would-be Shimmerer, a 0/0 frame (record 4). gather: source 1a finds Shimmerer's own "enters with three" (SourceOnly), and the sweep finds Biomancer's. Doubling Season's pattern watches counters being put, and the proposal carries none, so pattern_watches says no and it is not a candidate. Candidates: Biomancer and Shimmerer, on one step of the ladder (CR 616.1e).
  2. ordering_cannot_change_outcome. shared_clauses_hold ✓: both mandatory, static, no rider. EntryWrites::of_candidates state: both write +1/+1, so raises_power; the Mutant edit only adds; the entering object has no static that reads its own counters, so not feeds_all. classify: Biomancer is ModsAdding { kinds: [+1/+1], status: None }, because its amount reads Biomancer, not the entering object, and a raised power cannot unmatch "other creatures you control". Shimmerer is ModsAdding too. commutes(ModsAdding, ModsAdding) → true. Before RG the answer stopped here: suppressed, and the first candidate, Biomancer, applied.
  3. counters_invite_a_multiplier, premise (d). Two members write counters. Build the entry as it would stand after both, with one counter of each kind each writes, and gather against it. That is record 6, a look-ahead of a 2/2 frame, taken for this question only. Doubling Season matches now: Amount(Multiplier(2)), not a member, not yet applied, watching +1/+1, which both members write. So the order is observable.
  4. The question, record 7: ChooseReplacementEffect, options [Biomancer, Shimmerer], player 0 player. Shimmerer first: counters: [+1/+1 × 3].
  5. Iteration 2: a 3/3 frame (record 9). Candidates: Biomancer, and now Doubling Season. classify: Doubling Season is a Multiplier and Biomancer is ModsAdding. commutes passes that pair only on disjoint kinds, and both are +1/+1. That is RE-5's cell, unchanged. Asked (record 10): Doubling Season first, 3 × 2 = 6.
  6. Iteration 3: a 6/6 frame (record 12). Biomancer alone, single: +2 and the Mutant. Iteration 4: an 8/8 frame (record 14), no candidates. Performed with 8.

Why the pairwise table could not see it. commutes compares members with each other, and Doubling Season was not a member: it only becomes applicable once one of them has written (CR 616.2). The question is not "do these two commute?" but "does anything waiting on their writes multiply them?" That takes a look at an entry that does not exist yet, which is why premise (d) is one more gather, run only when two or more members put counters on the entry. A plus (Hardened Scales) is exempt: it adds its one amount once whenever it applies, so no order moves it.
Found in the build, not designed. RE-5's planeswalker test failed once CR 306.5b stopped being a seed (trace C) and became a member. The bug was older than RG, and registered cards reached it. On the pooled boards, one four-seat stress game in 200 asks it, with Primal Vigor as the doubler.

Trace C — Kaito beside Oath of Gideon, both orders

The fixture is Kaito, Bane of Nightmares' decisive clause: a planeswalker with loyalty 4 that, as long as it has a loyalty counter, is a 3/4 Ninja creature (the card adds "during your turn", which the fixture drops). His seventh ruling says that while he's a creature he is not a planeswalker. Oath of Gideon: each planeswalker you control enters with an additional loyalty counter.

  1. Iteration 1: EntryFrame → compute_as_entering. The would-be Kaito has no counters, so his static's Condition::SourceHasCounters is false and he is a planeswalker frame (record 4). At the end of layer 4, add_intrinsic_entry_abilities tests the frame's types (a mask on CardTypes since the review) and puts CR 306.5b's ability on the frame. It is a static "enters with 4 loyalty counters" with the id derived_on(Kaito, SynthesizedAbility::PlaneswalkerLoyalty): the candidate id in record 7 ends in …205, object 2 and tag 5.
  2. gather: source 1a reads that frame's abilities and finds the synthesized one (SourceOnly), as it would find a printed "enters with". The sweep finds Oath, whose filter "planeswalkers you control" reads the same frame frame: a planeswalker, so a candidate.
  3. ordering_cannot_change_outcome: EntryWrites::of_candidates sees both write counters, and characteristics_hang_on_entry_state(frame) is true, because Kaito has a static whose condition reads his counters (Condition::reads_entry_state). That is the feeds table's last row: any counter write can change any characteristic, so feeds_all. classify(Oath): its filter can_unmatch, so it has no class, and the order is asked player.
  4. C1, CR 306.5b first (record 6, answer 1): 4 loyalty counters. Iteration 2: the would-be Kaito has counters, so he is a 3/4 creature and not a planeswalker frame (record 8). The synthesis finds no planeswalker; Oath's filter reads a creature. No candidates. He enters with 4.
  5. C2, Oath first (answer 0): 1 loyalty counter. Iteration 2: a 3/4 creature again. This time it is CR 306.5b that is gone: the frame is not a planeswalker, so the synthesis puts no ability on it and source 1a has nothing to find. He enters with 1. He never enters with none, and never with 5.
Before RG, default_enter_mods seeded the 4 counters into the proposal before the loop ran. Iteration 1's frame was already a creature, Oath never applied, and nothing was asked: always 4, the order CR 616.1 offers skipped. The same seed is why a planeswalker creature under Humility entered with its counters. Now Humility's layer 6 takes the synthesized ability like any other, it enters with none, and CR 704.5i puts it into the graveyard.
The gray area this board sits on. Steps C1 and C2 follow CR 614.12's text: the look-ahead counts "replacement effects that have already modified how it enters", including the counters they add. Arixmethes, Slumbering Isle's 2020 ruling implies the opposite, that an entering permanent's "enters with" counters don't change what the look-ahead sees. Under that reading Kaito stays a planeswalker through the loop and gets 5, and he would enter untapped under an opponent's Authority of the Consuls, where the engine taps him. WotC's rules management called it a contradiction in 2024, and the most recent staff statement (2025) reads Arixmethes by 614.12's text. The one place that decides it: whether the look-ahead's condition checks read the counters on the would-be entity (Condition::SourceHasCounters on the entering object). The engine follows the CR's text, and the decision is recorded with its evidence.

What RG changed, trace by trace, and what is still open

A is the shape. What an entry writes is either state the permanent carries or characteristics it entered as. Both are built by one function for the performer and the look-ahead, and neither is a registry row. That is why the Mutant outlives the ability that made it.

B is the finding. Suppressing CR 616.1's question needs more than "these two commute": nothing waiting on their writes can be allowed to multiply them. The table in §3.5 now states four premises and what holds each.

C is CR 306.5b as an ability. Moving it off the seed and onto the frame is what makes Oath's order real, lets Humility remove it, and exposed B.

Open, each with a dated line in codebase-state.md. Item 185's other elisions still stand on arguments written beside their sites. Item 187: a tap bumps no layer epoch while a layer row can read the tapped status. Item 188: the review's survey of two roads to one state, where the repeated cleanup step never ends "until end of turn" effects. The gray area in trace C is decided by the CR's text. And the dependency re-check that makes a Mutant cost Urborg a hypothetical (the budget's reading) waits for a smarter CR 613.8 loop.
